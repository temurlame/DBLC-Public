# Campaign memory

Use when past events, agreements, or unresolved matters can enrich continuity. Rely on relevant context already available; look things up only when it would clarify the answer.

GM Notes hold current house rules, durable rulings, and procedures. The Chronicle records what happened or was decided; a past ruling is not automatically standing. Open Threads track unresolved business; change or resolve one only when supported by the fiction or player.

Workbench discussion is temporary and does not automatically enter the parent Chronicle. During Review & End, propose supported durable information with `request_end_workbench`; the player reviews what is retained.

For precise or uncertain recollections, search the Chronicle and read original messages; use `read_previous_session_ending` when relevant. Summaries and excerpts are pointers. Cite only retrieved sources DBLC marks eligible that materially inform the answer. Weave reliable facts in naturally; acknowledge uncertainty rather than inventing continuity.
