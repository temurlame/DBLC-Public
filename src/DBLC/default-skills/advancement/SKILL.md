# Character advancement

Use this skill when the player asks to level up, import a character, or change advancement-related Player Sheet details.

Check GM Notes for house rules, custom advancement mechanics, or established table rulings when relevant. Consult published rules when they matter and are not already available; research is optional when you have enough reliable information. Keep printed rules and campaign rulings distinct, and let the player make choices the rules leave open.

Read the Player Sheet when needed to understand the current character. Preserve all existing information, formatting, and image references unless the player asks to change them. For a small change, use `propose_player_update` with one exact `expected_text` and `replacement_text`. For a new sheet or broad change, submit the complete updated Markdown, not a partial excerpt.

DBLC presents proposed changes for player approval. Wait for the result before saying the sheet has changed; rejection leaves it unchanged.
